Privacy Policy

PadSampler for Android and Windows, by Nerd Instruments. Effective 16 November 2026.

In short

PadSampler does not collect, store, send or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. Everything you make stays on your device.

Your files

Samples you load, sessions, presets and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage (Android) or in %APPDATA%\PadSampler (Windows). They are never uploaded anywhere. You choose which files and folders the app may read, using the system file picker. When you export a session, the file goes only to the place you choose.

Network

PadSampler works offline. The only network use is Ableton Link, and only while you switch LINK on: it exchanges tempo, beat position and (if you enable Link Audio) the master audio with other Link apps on your local network. Nothing is sent to the internet or to us.

Google Play

When you install PadSampler from Google Play, the download is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. We receive no information about you, only anonymous totals that Google shows to every developer (such as the number of installs).

Donations

If you choose to support development, the payment is made on PayPal's website under PayPal's privacy statement. We receive only what PayPal shows to any payee (your name, email and the amount), use it only for our records and never share it.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) for security; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. Downloads of the Windows version are served by GitHub (GitHub's privacy statement).

Children

PadSampler collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
